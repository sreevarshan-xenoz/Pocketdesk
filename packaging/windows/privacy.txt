Pocketdesk and your privacy

Pocketdesk has no account and no Pocketdesk server, and collects nothing: no analytics, no crash reports, no update checks. What you send between your phone and this computer is encrypted end to end.

To reach this computer from outside your home network, Pocketdesk uses some public services by default:

- Relays run by n0, the company that builds iroh (*.relay.n0.iroh.link). They see both ends' public IP addresses and Pocketdesk ids, and when and how much they talk; never the content.
- The Mainline DHT, a public directory, so your phone finds this computer again after its address changes. Its nodes see this computer's public IP and its Pocketdesk id.
- Your home router, asked to open a port for a direct path.

To turn these off, open Pocketdesk's Settings after installing: "Reachable from the internet" off stops all three (phones then reach this computer only on the same network or through Tailscale); "Found by id (DHT)" set to off stops only the DHT; or list your own relay under "Your own relays".

On your own network, the computer announces itself so phones there find it ("Visible on this network" turns that off).

The full privacy policy: https://github.com/sreevarshan-xenoz/Pocketdesk/blob/main/docs/privacy.md
