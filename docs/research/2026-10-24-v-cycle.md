# The V cycle (2026-10-24), after U

The U cycle proved the T cycle on a Linux VM and polished the pages it touched. Two things it turned up are not fixed yet:
- the away lock counted a phone linked by the internet road as present, so a phone that left on mobile data never locked the computer;
- Home could show two cards about the same pause.

Of the 899 owed checks, 76 can run here, on this box and the OPPO, without touching the owner's power, lock or input. The S cycle ran that bucket once, and much has been built since. So this cycle fixes the two things found, then runs the "Here" bucket again (Phase R: prove what exists before adding more). It removes this plan when it ends.

Each item is a branch and a PR (fixes found get their own), with its design and review in the PR and its entry in `docs/status-history.md`.

| # | Item | Where | Checkable here |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | The away lock counts only a link on this computer's network: the internet road and a tailnet reach from anywhere | The OPPO, a throwaway server with the lock seam | Yes |
| V2 | One card on Home when Android pauses Pocketdesk: the reminder steps aside for the health card, which takes its "Allow" | The OPPO | Yes |
| V3 | The "Here" owed checks (`docs/owed-checks.md`), in batches: run, then tick or rewrite to what they showed | The OPPO, throwaway servers | Yes |
| V4 | The desktop pages' layout checks U4 left open, against a throwaway server's real state: the Phone, Files and Devices pages from the snapshot harness | `desktop/` snapshots, a throwaway server | Yes |

## Not in it

- T9, a second screen and one window on GNOME and KDE: it needs one of those desktops to build against (the T cycle's end entry has its mapping).
- The owner-gated list carries over: the phone as a webcam, one-time codes, picture messages and group texts, a browser link for devices without the app, receiving from Quick Share, and unlocking with the phone's fingerprint.
