# The AA cycle (2026-10-24), after Z

The Z cycle ended at two things the phone does not say: that it put a copy from the computer on its clipboard, so the clipboard's echo check cannot show the phone took it, and what became of a share it was handed, so a share that never reaches the computer (one did, in X1) cannot be traced. This cycle adds those lines to the phone's log, never the content, and uses them (Phase R: prove what exists before adding more). It removes this plan when it ends.

Each item is a branch and a PR (fixes found get their own), with its design and review in the PR and its entry in `docs/status-history.md`.

| # | Item | Where | Checkable here |
|---|---|---|---|
| AA1 | A line when the phone puts text on its clipboard (how long, never the text), and `z1-echo` then requiring the phone took the copy | The OPPO, a throwaway server | Yes |
| AA2 | Lines along a share from the phone (handed over, to which computer and by which road, sent or why not), never a file's name, and the runner's share checks quoting them when one is lost | The OPPO, a throwaway server | Yes |
