# The next cycle: the phone's automation apps start the computer's tasks, a moved computer found again, and filters for what crosses (2026-10-22, O)

- N8 ended as a spike.

Sources, each gap checked against the code on `main` at 5b32967a:

## Where we are

- **Phone to computer.** The phone reaches the computer for nearly everything:
  - sessions, the remote, casts the computer drives, typing;
  - files and folders both ways, the phone's folders as a drive that now takes files;
  - the clipboard both ways;
  - notifications with answers, opens, clears, pictures and live updates;
  - calls, texts, music both ways in each side's media controls;
  - the phone's state and switches, commands to its automation apps, Home Assistant.
- **Linux** now has MPRIS, the laser dot, and the typing and cast windows checked on a VM.
- **Gaps found this time, each checked for this plan:**
  - **The phone's automation apps cannot start anything on the computer.** `docs/phone-automation.md` covers Connect, Wake, the clipboard and files only, and nothing exports a task or deck action (KDE bugs 512641, 519904).
  - **The computer cannot take a screenshot of the phone or lock it without a cast.** C8's control service never uses `GLOBAL_ACTION_TAKE_SCREENSHOT` or `GLOBAL_ACTION_LOCK_SCREEN` (scrcpy #6682, #6426).
  - **A paired computer at a new address shows as an unknown one.** `FoundComputers.merge` matches by address alone (F6 chose that, since a name or id can be spoofed), and pins are kept per address. A changed DHCP lease, the phone's hotspot or USB tethering all lose the card (KDE bug 524452; KDE Discuss 44308).
  - **Nothing keeps a copy from one program, or one that looks like a secret, off the phone.** Only the private marks are honoured, and password apps do not always set them (KDE bug 519109; KDE Discuss 51056).
  - **A notification can be shared or not only by its app,** not by its kind (Android's channel), so a chat app's "Updating messages…" comes along with its messages (KDE bug 508971).
  - **Several players on the computer:** the phone's controls reach whichever the system picks (KDE bug 509318).
  - **Received images never go onto the clipboard** unless they are F3's photos or T10h's screenshots (LocalSend #2682).
  - **Polish:**
    - the phone's Settings has no search, though it has about 35 switch rows (the desktop's got J2's);
    - Diagnostics checks the firewall on Windows only (KDE bug 518113 on Linux).
  - **For the owner's SIM:**
    - calls: answer, hang up, and pause the computer's music while one rings (KDE bug 526013; F5 declines only);
    - texts: a code's Copy button (KDE bug 520107), the SIM a reply goes on (KDE Discuss 37338), a text read on the computer cleared on the phone (GSConnect #2014).

The owner's standing rules hold:
- every feature's knobs go in Settings on both sides;
- anything that reads or sends more is off by default;
- never power, lock or server-stop actions on the owner's box;
- the OPPO is a test phone, free to drive by adb;
- no macOS or iOS.

## O1. The phone's automation apps start the computer's tasks and deck buttons

**What:** two exported actions, as T7b's are.
- **`dev.pocketdesk.android.action.RUN_TASK`** takes a computer's address and a task's name. It starts the task as the Tasks sheet does, and an ask-first task still asks at the desk.
- **`dev.pocketdesk.android.action.PRESS_DECK`** takes an address and a button's label. It presses the button as the deck sheet does.

**The rules:**
- Both are behind the phone's Settings "Let other apps run the computer's tasks", off by default, since any app can send them.
- Only to a saved computer.
- The answer goes back as a result broadcast that F1's events already carry (`TASK_FINISHED`) and to the log.
- Nothing changes on the computer: its permissions for Tasks and Input hold.

**Checked:** `adb am` with a throwaway server, a refusal with the switch off, an unsaved address.

## O2. A screenshot of the phone, and lock it, from the computer

**What:**
- **Screenshot.** The Overview's and the tray's "Screenshot of the phone" asks the phone (`kdeconnect.pocketdesk.phone_action {screenshot}`). With C8's control service on, the phone takes it with `GLOBAL_ACTION_TAKE_SCREENSHOT`. T10h's watcher then sends it as it sends any new screenshot, or a share of it when T10h is off.
- **Lock.** "Lock the phone" uses `GLOBAL_ACTION_LOCK_SCREEN`.
- **Permissions.** A per-phone permission, Phone actions (on), on the computer. The phone's own switch, "Let the computer take screenshots and lock this phone", is off by default.
- **Never while the phone is locked,** and 10 a minute.

**Checked:** on the OPPO with a throwaway server.

## O3. A paired computer found again at a new address

**What:** a found computer whose announcement names a saved computer's id is probed at its new address with the connection doctor's compare-only identity check (`probe_identity`). Only when its certificate matches the saved pin does the card follow:
- the address is updated;
- the pin is copied to the new address;
- a snackbar says so.

A spoofed name never moves a card. With the phone's hotspot or USB tethering on, the phone also probes its own sharing subnet for a saved computer's control port, which the doctor already reads.

**Checked:** a second loopback address by `adb reverse` on another port. The hotspot needs the owner's computer on it, so it is owed.

## O4. Copies kept from the phone by program or by look

**What:** desktop Settings "Never send copies from" lists programs, and "Never send copies that look like" lists patterns (regexes; a sample of a password manager's and a key's are offered). A copy that matches stays on the computer:
- for T10b's push;
- for D3's history;
- for a session's sync.

Windows names the copying program by `GetClipboardOwner`'s process. Linux has no owner to read, so only the patterns apply there.

**Checked:** the clipboard seam, and a live Windows owner test.

## O5. Notifications shared by their kind

**What:**
- **The phone's Settings** list each shared app's channels (Android's `NotificationChannel` names, through the listener's `getNotificationChannels` for a companion, else the channels seen so far). Each kind can be turned off.
- **`NotificationForwardRule`** drops a notification whose channel is off.

**Checked:** the fixture's two channels.

## O6. The computer's players

**What:** the phone's Sound sheet, N5's media controls and the widget get "Player" with the sessions the computer lists (GSMTC's sessions, `playerctl -l`). The choice is kept per computer, and the media requests name it; with none chosen, the system's own is used, as now.

**Checked:** the media seam with two players. A press to a real player needs the owner.

## O7. Received images onto the clipboard

**What:** the computer's Settings "Images phones send also go onto the clipboard" (off), and the phone's "Images from the computer also go onto my clipboard" (off). A received PNG or JPEG under 32 MB is put on the clipboard as N4's are.

## O8. Find a setting on the phone

**What:** a search field at the top of the phone's Settings. It matches each row's title, its summary and its group's name, as J2's rule does on the desktop (`SettingsSearch`, JVM-tested). Groups with no match are hidden.

## O9. Diagnostics' firewall row on Linux

**What:** `check_firewall` on Linux reads `ufw status` or `firewall-cmd --list-all` within 3 s and says whether TCP 1716 and UDP 4716 are open. Neither present is "no firewall found", never a failure.

**Checked:** on the VM with ufw.

## O10. Calls and texts for the owner's SIM

**What:**
- **Calls:**
  - Answer and Hang up beside F5's Decline (`acceptRingingCall`, `endCall`);
  - "Pause the computer's music while a call rings", off.
- **Texts:**
  - a "Copy code" button on a text's toast and the Messages page;
  - the reply sent on the SIM the text came on, with a SIM choice for a new text;
  - a text read on the computer cleared on the phone.

**Checked:** nothing here; built when the owner has a SIM in a test phone.

## Considered and left out

- **The Windows share sheet:** it needs C9's package identity, which the owner skipped.
- **Bluetooth as a road, Wi-Fi Direct or Aware:** large, and the hotspot and the internet road cover the case.
- **The phone's media volume from the computer:** Android 17 ignores background volume calls.
- **A sleep timer:** one request, and a task or deck button already does it.
- **A companion-device association (Android 17's exemption from the three-hour code delay):** its permission level for ordinary apps is unconfirmed, and it needs the computer's Bluetooth discoverable (the owner's OK) and an Android 17 phone. Kept as a note for C11.
- **Any language typed on GNOME or KDE while uinput is open:** it needs a GNOME/KDE VM, so it waits for the next Linux pass.
