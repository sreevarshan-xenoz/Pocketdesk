# The R cycle (2026-10-23), after Q

2026's requests again found little that Pocketdesk lacks:
- **KDE Connect:** microphone sharing, a gamepad, pinch zoom, a drawing tablet, an intent listener.
- **Phone Link:** folding into Windows' shell and clipboard history, a Messages app of its own.
- **LocalSend:** a share-sheet entry, a Quick Settings tile, text from the command line.

Pocketdesk has each of these. So this cycle finishes what is half-done: the O cycle's checks by script, the two Linux rows that still say "no", the states the computer cannot see, and one word for "computer" in Spanish. It removes this plan when it ends.

## R1. The O cycle's device checks, run by script

**What:** `scripts/device_checks.py` gains a check for each O item a script can drive against its throwaway server:
- **O1:** `RUN_TASK` and `PRESS_DECK` broadcasts from adb, the task's and the button's answer on the server.
- **O5:** a fixture notification on a channel kept on the phone does not reach the computer; one on another does.
- **O6:** the computer's player choice, through `POCKETDESK_MEDIA_FILE`.

A check whose switch or service is off SKIPs with its reason, as before.

**Checked:** run on the OPPO.

## R2. What the computer cannot see yet

**What:**
- The phone's switches report gains M4's typing and M10's copies by themselves (Q3 left them out).
- The Overview says when "While presenting" holds notifications now: the server writes its quiet state, and the app reads it.

**Checked:** the report on the OPPO against a throwaway server; the Overview through the harness.

## R3. Lock when the phone leaves, on Linux

**What:** D9's lock on Linux. The keyboard's and mouse's idle time comes from GNOME (`org.gnome.Mutter.IdleMonitor`), KDE (`org.freedesktop.ScreenSaver`) or wlroots (`ext-idle-notify-v1`), and the lock is `loginctl lock-session`. A desktop that says nothing never locks, as now.

**Checked:** through `POCKETDESK_LOCK_FILE`, type-checked for Linux; GNOME, KDE and Sway owed.

## R4. Send the clipboard with a key, on Linux

**What:** H3's key on Linux through the GlobalShortcuts portal (KDE Plasma 6, GNOME 48 and later, Hyprland's portal), registered by the tray program. Where no portal offers it, Settings says so.

**Checked:** type-checked for Linux; the desktops owed.

## R5. One word for "computer" in Spanish

**What:** the phone app's Spanish says "equipo" (Windows' own word) everywhere, where it said "ordenador" in about 180 places and "equipo" in about 170. The Tamil is read for the same split.

**Checked:** on the OPPO in Spanish, by `uiautomator`.
