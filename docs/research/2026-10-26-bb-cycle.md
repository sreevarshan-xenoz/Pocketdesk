# The BB cycle (2026-10-26), after BA

AY made an unpaired phone's standing follow it on the computer, and BA closed what it had going there. This cycle audits the phone's side of the same unpair: the computer unpaired the phone (its Devices page, `pocketdesk unpair`, or a guest grant ending) while the phone still has the computer saved. The audit found that outside a session the phone never learns it: on the computer's network the server keeps an unpaired phone's connection, so it can pair on it, and drops what it sends without a word, so the phone goes on sending private content and counting it delivered. Each item is its own PR (Phase R). The plan is removed when the cycle ends.

| # | Item | Where | Checkable here |
|---|---|---|---|
| BB1 | The computer says nothing to a phone it refuses on the control link. It is told "not paired" once a connection (a packet of its own), as a session already is; the bridge keeps it per computer, until the phone pairs there again | `server`, `android/bridge` | Yes (unit tests; the runner, an unpair on the throwaway) |
