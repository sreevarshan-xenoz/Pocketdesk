# The BF cycle (2026-10-26): new features

AX to BD were reliability work. The owner asked for new, unique features again. An inventory of what Pocketdesk does and a look at KDE Connect, Phone Link, Continuity, Join, Unison, Moonlight and RustDesk found where the gaps are:
- something to tell you a long job on the computer has ended;
- a glance at the screen without a session;
- an approval from your own phone with no account or subscription;
- a quick note into the computer;
- a deck that follows what you are doing.

Each item is its own PR. Each lives behind a setting on both apps (the owner's rule) and is unit-tested; a short device check goes on the owed list. Long runs wait for several of these together, and none plays sound. The plan is removed when the cycle ends.

| # | Item | Where | Checkable here |
|---|---|---|---|
| BF1 | **Tell me when it's done.** `pocketdesk done -- <command>` runs a command, or `pocketdesk done --pid <n>` waits on one already running. When it ends, the phone gets a notification with the command, its exit status, how long it took, and its last lines. A long build, render, download or backup can be left while you are on the couch or out (by the internet road). | `server`, `core` | Yes (unit tests; the runner, a notification on the OPPO) |
| BF2 | **Peek.** The computer's screen as one still, on Home or a widget, with no session: "did the render finish?" in a glance. Each phone has a permission for it, off by default; it honours privacy mode; it refreshes on a tap. | `server`, `android/app`, `android/bridge` | Yes (unit tests; the OPPO) |
| BF3 | **Approve from the phone.** `pocketdesk ask "<question>"` shows the question on the phone, answered with Yes or No behind the phone's fingerprint or screen lock. It returns 0 for yes, 1 for no, 2 for no answer in time, so a script, a deploy or git hook can wait on the owner's say. | `server`, `core`, `android/app` | Yes (unit tests; the OPPO, answered by adb) |
| BF4 | **Note to the computer.** The share sheet, a Quick Settings tile and a deck button append text or a link to a Markdown inbox on the computer (a file the owner picks in Settings), with the time. Off until a file is picked. | `server`, `desktop`, `android/app` | Yes (unit tests; the OPPO) |
| BF5 | **A deck that follows the app.** A deck page can be tied to a program (OBS, VS Code, a browser). The phone's deck turns to that page when that program comes to the front on the computer. On Windows and Hyprland. | `server`, `desktop`, `android/app` | Yes (unit tests) |
| BF6 | **Polish.** The new pages and cards, on both apps: layout, empty states, wording, dark mode, TalkBack, Spanish and Tamil. | `desktop`, `android/app` | Yes (UI tests; renders) |

## Not in it

- **Send this window to my phone**, **deliver later**, **copy text or a QR from the screen**, **a desk dock**, **continue where I left off**, **presenter view**, **a pocket terminal**, **the phone as a webcam**: for a later cycle.
- **Pausing media on a call**: it needs sound to check (the owner said no sound testing).
- **Unlocking the computer from the phone**: it needs lock tests on the owner's computer (never).
- "Pocketdesk control", the owner-gated list, and the 0.7.0 tag.
