# The AJ cycle (2026-10-25), after AI

The cycles since AA ran nearly every owed check this computer and the OPPO can reach, and fixed what they found. This cycle is the loop's polish step for the phone app: every Pocketdesk screen seen as a person sees it, from screenshots on the OPPO, in light and dark, and in Spanish and Tamil where words run longest, and what is wrong fixed. Screenshots stay on this computer, never in the repo. It removes this plan when it ends.

Each fix is a branch and a PR, with its before and after in the PR and its entry in `docs/status-history.md`.
