# The next cycle: a computer that follows its network, apps opened on the phone, and rules for what reaches the desk (2026-10-22, P)

Date: 2026-10-22, after the O cycle (`docs/research/2026-10-22-o-cycle.md`). O1–O9 are built:
- O1–O3 merged.
- O4–O9 are in review.
- O10 waits for the owner's SIM.

Sources:
- **KDE's bug tracker:** kdeconnect bugs opened since 2026-01-01, 181 of them.
- **KDE Discuss:** the kdeconnect tag.
- **Issue trackers:** GSConnect, LocalSend and scrcpy.
- **Android:** Android 17's features and behavior changes.

Each candidate was checked against the code. Each item is checked again as it starts.

## Where we are

- **What the O cycle added:**
  - automation apps start tasks;
  - a screenshot and a lock from the computer;
  - a moved computer found again;
  - copies kept by program or by look;
  - notifications by their kind;
  - the computer's player chosen;
  - received images onto the clipboard;
  - the phone's Settings search;
  - Linux's firewall row.
- **Gaps found this time:**
  - **The computer's announcement goes stale.** It is made once with the addresses of the moment. After a router change, sleep or a VPN, phones on the network cannot find it until it restarts (`core/src/tailscale/lan.rs`; KDE bugs 524179, 526688, 514470, 521261).
  - **Nothing opens an app on the phone from the computer.** No package list and no launch exist; N8 ended as a note since casting cannot pick one app (scrcpy #6945, #6858, #7048).
  - **Phone toasts follow only manual pauses.** There is no schedule, and Windows' presenting or full-screen state does not hold them (K2a's `pause.rs`; KDE Discuss 50868, bugs 404043, 383384).
  - **A notification can be held by its app (K2b), but not by its words** (KDE Discuss 49713, GSConnect #1838).
  - **A ringing alarm on the phone likely never reaches the computer.** It is ongoing, which `NotificationForwardRule` drops (KDE bug 436980). To be confirmed on the OPPO first.
  - **Files go to one phone or computer at a time** (LocalSend #3630, KDE bug 438505).
  - **Files copied in Explorer are not sent** by the clipboard key or the tray's Send the clipboard (LocalSend #3478, #3499; KDE bugs 392164, 522083).
  - **Every link a phone sends opens in the default browser,** with no rule by site (KDE Discuss 48960, KDE bug 527115).
  - **Permissions are set phone by phone,** from M2b's three presets, and cannot be copied (KDE Discuss 51056, 48807; KDE bugs 422769, 525654).
  - **The computer cannot browse the phone's contacts.** T8e names a text's sender only (GSConnect #2155; KDE Discuss 16407).
  - **Ring only sounds.** It neither vibrates nor flashes the torch (KDE bug 376153).

The owner's standing rules hold:
- every feature's knobs go in Settings on both sides;
- anything that reads or sends more is off by default;
- never power, lock or server-stop actions on the owner's box;
- the OPPO is a test phone, free to drive by adb;
- no macOS or iOS;
- AGENTS.md stays small, with each item's entry in `docs/status-history.md`;
- build leftovers are cleared as items merge.

## P1. The computer's announcement follows its network

**What:** the server reads its addresses every 30 s and when the system says the network changed, and announces again on a change. It withdraws the old record with an mDNS goodbye. A wake from sleep counts as a change.

**Checked:** a unit test of the address diff, and an announcement seen again with a new address through a seam (`POCKETDESK_LOCAL_IPS`). The owner's network card is never touched.

## P2. Open an app on the phone from the computer

**What:**
- **The phone's side:** Settings "Let the computer open my apps" (off). The phone answers its own computer's ask with its launchable apps, names and ids only.
- **The computer's side:** "Open an app on the phone…" on the Overview and in the tray lists them, with a search box.
- **The launch:** the phone opens the one picked, unlocked and awake, with N3's background start while C8's control service is bound, else as "Tap to open". This is a per-phone permission, Phone actions.

**Checked:** on the OPPO by adb; the list, an app opened, a refusal while locked.

## P3. Quiet hours on a schedule, and while presenting

**What:**
- **Quiet hours:** desktop Settings "Quiet hours" (a start and an end, the days; off) holds phones' notifications as K2a's pause does: logged and kept to answer, no toast.
- **While presenting:** "While presenting or in full screen" (off) does the same while Windows says so (`SHQueryUserNotificationState`). Linux takes the schedule only.

**Checked:** the schedule's rule by unit test, and the state through a seam.

## P4. Notifications held by their words

**What:** desktop Settings "Hold notifications with these words" (one per line, an app optional; off when empty) holds a matching phone notification as K2b's mute does. It is logged with the rule that held it, and Activity says so.

**Checked:** unit tests, and the fixture app's notifications against a throwaway server.

## P5. A ringing alarm or timer on the computer

**What:** first confirmed on the OPPO, that a ringing alarm is dropped as ongoing. If so, an alarm or a finished timer (`CATEGORY_ALARM`, or the clock app's ongoing alert) goes to the computer as an urgent toast. Its own Snooze and Dismiss are answered on T10c's road. This is behind the phone's "Alarms on the computer" (off).

**Checked:** `adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.SET_ALARM`, and the toast against a throwaway server.

## P6. Files to several phones or computers at once

**What:** the Files page's "To" takes several targets, or "every linked one". `pocketdesk send` takes several. Each target gets its own offer and its own row in the sent list.

**Checked:** two throwaway servers and the OPPO.

## P7. Files copied in Explorer sent with the clipboard key

**What:** when the clipboard holds files (`CF_HDROP`; `text/uri-list` on Linux), H3's key and the tray's Send the clipboard send those files to the phone, as Send file… does. On the Files page, Ctrl+V takes them as a drop.

**Checked:** through a throwaway clipboard seam, never the owner's own.

## P8. Links by site

**What:** desktop Settings "Links from phones" gains rules: a site (a host, or its end) → a program, the browser, or "ask". T8a's checks stay. A link that fails to open is said, not silent.

**Checked:** unit tests of the match, and a throwaway server with a stand-in opener.

## P9. Permission profiles

**What:** the Devices page saves a phone's permissions as a named profile, applies one to another phone, and offers the profiles at pairing beside M2b's three.

**Checked:** unit tests and the harness.

## P10. The phone's contacts on the computer

**What:**
- **The phone's side:** Settings "My contacts on the computer" (off). It answers its own computer's ask with names and numbers only, asking Android for contacts as T8e does.
- **The computer's side:** the Messages page gains a searchable list with Copy, Text and Call (Call through L4).

**Checked:** contacts seeded by adb on the OPPO. Texts and calls stay owed to the SIM.

## P11. Ring that vibrates and flashes

**What:** N1's ring also vibrates in a pattern and flashes the torch while it sounds, each with its own switch under "Ring from the computer" (on). Both stop with Stop.

**Checked:** on the OPPO.

## Not taken

- **Locating the phone on a map:** a sensitive new permission, on weak evidence of need.
- **Bluetooth discovery and transfer:** it needs a radio on both ends.
- **A virtual webcam (F6 part 2):** it waits for the owner's OK.
- **Android TV:** it needs new hardware.
- **A Windows share-sheet entry:** it needs a package identity (C9, skipped).
- **Android 17's Handoff API:** it moves activities between Android devices only.
