# The next cycle: checks that run themselves, the phone's music and live updates anywhere, and notifications that open (2026-10-21, N)

Date: 2026-10-21, after the M cycle (`docs/research/2026-10-21-m-cycle.md`). M1–M10 are merged, and M4, M6, M8 and M10 were checked on the OPPO the same evening (checklist §170, §171, §173, §175).

Sources:
- KDE's bug tracker and KDE Discuss (2025–2026);
- Microsoft's Phone Link support pages and Windows 11's File Explorer for phones;
- Apple's iPhone Mirroring and Live Activities on macOS Tahoe (as what users now expect);
- Android 14's app screen sharing and Android 16's Live Updates;
- scrcpy and KDE Connect release notes.

Each gap was checked against the code on `main` at f890353b.

## Where we are

- **The phone and the computer reach each other for nearly everything:** sessions, the remote, casts the computer can drive, typing without a cast, files and folders, the clipboard both ways (now by itself from the phone), notifications with answers, clears and pictures, calls, texts, the phone's state and switches, commands, Home Assistant.
- **Gaps the repo shows, each checked against the code for this plan:**
  - **The owed checks are run by hand each cycle,** with a throwaway chat app made again each time (checklist §115, §120, §164). `docs/owed-checks.md` lists 837 open checks, 509 of them runnable here; nothing runs them.
  - **The phone's music reaches the computer only in a session** (`media/PhoneMediaEffect.kt` is called only from the session screen), and with no cover art.
  - **A phone notification on the computer cannot open its app on the phone.** Nothing reads a notification's `contentIntent`.
  - **Ongoing notifications never reach the computer** (`NotificationForwardRule.kt` drops every ongoing one): no download progress, no timer, no Android 16 Live Update.
  - **The computer's music has no media controls on the phone's lock screen or headset:** the widget (G5) and tile (G6) only.
  - **The phone's drive is read-only** (D8 answers PUT and MKCOL with 405).
  - **Every computer of mine toasts** (L8 left "only where I am working" out).
  - **A cast shares the whole screen;** Android 14 QPR2 can share one app, which the cast never asks for.
  - **Small leftovers:** typing from the tray or a key (M4 part 2), several links in one share, a warning when the phone is nearly full (L9).
  - **96 owed checks are Linux-only,** and K7's MPRIS player and G4's laser were never built for Linux.

The owner's standing rules hold:
- every feature's knobs go in Settings on both sides;
- anything that reads or sends more is off by default;
- never power, lock or server-stop actions on the owner's box;
- the OPPO is a test phone, free to drive by adb;
- no macOS or iOS.

## N1. Owed checks that run by script

**What:**
- **A fixture app** (`android/fixture/`, its own Gradle module, never in the release APK). It:
  - posts MessagingStyle notifications with Reply and Mark as read, progress notes and call-like notes;
  - copies text or a PNG;
  - plays a tone;
  - runs a MediaSession;
  - writes a geotagged JPEG.
- **A runner** (`scripts/device_checks.py`). It:
  - starts a throwaway server through the config and state seams, the phone's input off in `trusted_devices.json`;
  - drives the phone by adb;
  - checks each result through the seams: the clipboard file, downloads folder, phone audio WAV, the event log and `uiautomator dump`;
  - prints PASS, FAIL or TIMEOUT for each checklist id, with a deadline on every wait.
- **The first batch:** about 30 checks the K–M cycles repeated by hand: notifications and answers, clears, the clipboard both ways, files with their MD5, links, the battery, M3, M5, M6, M10.

**Why:** the owner's rule batches device checks, 509 can run here, and each cycle has re-made the same throwaway app.

**Exists now:** `scripts/owed_checks.py` (an index), the server's test seams, the desktop harness (C4). No adb runner, no committed fixture.

**Risks:**
- **The phone's state:** connected tests uninstall the app, so the runner never runs them without `leaveApksInstalledAfterRun`. Its pairings with the throwaway are its own.
- **ColorOS:** a force-stop turns the Accessibility service off (found 2026-10-21), and installs wait on its check screen. The runner says so rather than hanging.

## N2. The phone's music on the computer without a session, with cover art

**What:**
- **On the phone:** with M1's link kept, the notification listener reports the playing media session (T8d's `phone_media`) over the background link, from a session-change callback (no polling), with a small JPEG of the cover once per track.
- **On the computer:** the Overview, the tray, K7's media controls and the Sessions page show it without a session; presses go through `phone_media_out/` as now.

**Settings:**
- the phone: "My media on the computer in the background", off;
- the computer: K7's row.

**Why:**
- Phone Link's audio player works whenever the phone is linked (Microsoft's support page).
- KDE bug 400030.
- K7's own spec left out a phone outside a session, and art.

**Risks:**
- **Battery:** measure with N7's script.
- **Privacy:** titles leave the phone in the background, so it is off by default.

## N3. "Open on the phone"

**What:** each phone notification's toast, and its row on the Notifications page, gets "Open on the phone".
- **On the phone:** it fires that notification's `contentIntent` under C8's bound Accessibility service, which may start activities from the background; without the service it posts "Tap to open" instead. Only for a notification sent to that computer, never while locked.
- **Optional:** with "Show it here" on, a cast starts so the app shows in the phone's window.

**Settings:**
- the phone: "Let the computer open my notifications", off;
- the computer: a per-phone permission.

**Why:**
- Apple's iPhone Mirroring opens a clicked notification's app (macOS Tahoe 26.4).
- Phone Link opens the app on Samsung phones only (Microsoft Tech Community, 2026-09-05).

**Risks:**
- Android 14's limits on background starts (measure on the OPPO).
- The cast's consent each time.
- A new reach, so a SECURITY.md limit.

## N4. The phone's live updates on the computer

**What:** ongoing notifications from opted-in apps (progress, chronometers, Android 16's ProgressStyle), sent as one updating card. The computer shows a Windows toast with a progress binding under one tag, and the Notifications page.

**Limits:**
- at most one update every 5 s, or on a 5 % change;
- never Pocketdesk's own notifications, foreground-service "running" notes, or media (N2's).

**Settings:**
- the phone: "Ongoing updates", off, per app;
- the computer: on.

**Why:** Android 16's Live Updates (OPPO among the partners), and macOS Tahoe's Live Activities from the iPhone.

**Risks:**
- Chatter and battery.
- Navigation notes say where the owner is, and the Settings line says so.

## N5. The computer's music in the phone's media controls

**What:** a MediaSession and MediaStyle notification on the phone for the last computer's player, with art. It shows on the lock screen and in the shade, and answers headset buttons and a watch.

- **The server pushes changes** (GSMTC events, MPRIS) on the link; T8c only answers asks.
- **Art** comes from the GSMTC thumbnail or `mpris:artUrl`.

**Settings:**
- the phone: "Computer's music in my media controls", off;
- the computer: under the Audio permission.

**Why:** KDE bug 337485, and KDE Discuss's "No album art" from Windows 11 (2025-01-11).

**Risks:**
- An echo with K7, so the phone's own session copy is never sent back.
- Headset buttons taken from the phone's own players.
- A press changes this box's media, so it is checked with the owner.

## N6. Files into the phone's folders from the computer's drive

**What:** D8's drive takes PUT and MKCOL into folders the phone shares with a new "the computer may add files" mark. The folder is re-granted for writing.

- **How a file lands:** through the share road into SAF as `.part`, renamed once whole (C2's way).
- **Never:** DELETE or overwrite. A taken name gets the next free one.

**Settings:**
- the phone: a per-folder mark, off;
- the computer: "The phone's folders take files".

**Why:** Windows 11's File Explorer copies both ways with an Android phone.

**Risks:** writing to the phone's storage is a new reach, so a SECURITY.md limit.

## N7. Toasts only on the computer in use

**What:** each computer tells its phone whether it is in use, from its input idle time:
- Windows: `GetLastInputInfo`, read as D9 does;
- Linux: `ext-idle-notify`.

The phone marks copies to idle computers as M6 marks `phoneInUse`. They land on the Notifications page with no toast. With no computer in use, every computer toasts.

**Settings:** the phone's "Toast only where I am working", off.

**Why:** both L8 specs left it out.

**Risks:** a stale reading, so after 30 s with no report every computer toasts.

## N8. One app on the computer (a spike first)

**What:** does ColorOS 14 offer Android 14 QPR2's "share one app" in the cast's consent? If it does:
- handle `onCapturedContentResize` and `onCapturedContentVisibilityChanged`;
- map C8's touches to the app's bounds.

If it does not, it ends as a note.

**Spike (2026-10-22): it does not.** The OPPO (CPH2359, ColorOS 14, `CPH2359_14.0.0.3101`, security patch 2026-09-01) shows Pocketdesk's "Show screen" consent as "Start recording or casting with Pocketdesk?" with Cancel and Start now only: no choice of one app, so N8 ends here. It is worth a look again on a phone with Android 14 QPR2's dialog, or Android 15.

## Considered and left out

- **K6, a link for a browser:** a LAN listener and a firewall rule, so the owner's OK.
- **A webcam and a second screen:** a driver or a camera device, so the owner's OK.
- **Windows' Cross-Device Resume:** apps must integrate it, and Android's media sessions carry no link.
- **Files on the clipboard:** no new request; drops (L2) and Send To (T10e) cover it.
- **Images in clipboard history:** D3 keeps text, and the cost is high.
- **The pointer moving onto the phone:** Android gives no app a pointer to drive; C8's gestures cover it.
- **scrcpy 4.x's camera controls:** F6's own checks are still owed.
- **The M plan's left-outs, unchanged:** RCS, call audio, Wi-Fi and hotspot toggles, the phone's volume and ringer from the computer, Windows notifications to the phone (a package identity), Wear OS, Android TV.

One limit on the sources: no 2026 tracker thread asks for N4 or N6 directly; both rest on what the platforms now do.
