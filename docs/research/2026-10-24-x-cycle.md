# The X cycle (2026-10-24), after W

The W cycle gave the device-check runner sessions and long transfers over the internet road, with the phone's input off. What is left of the owed checks that run here leans on two things it does not do yet: sending from the phone (its share sheet), and a transfer that is cut and then continued. This cycle adds those to the runner and runs what they open, with two short session checks from the server's log (Phase R: prove what exists before adding more). It removes this plan when it ends.

Each item is a branch and a PR (fixes found get their own), with its design and review in the PR and its entry in `docs/status-history.md`.

| # | Item | Where | Checkable here |
|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | The runner shares from the phone: a file pushed to it and shared to Pocketdesk (`ShareActivity`, a media content URI), to the throwaway; then a gigabyte from the phone with its MD5 (§31) and `file_received` at both detail levels (§78) | The OPPO, a throwaway server | Yes |
| X2 | A cut transfer continued, not sent again whole: a send to the phone cut part way, the link back, the same file again, and how much of it went the second time (§46, §50) | The OPPO, a throwaway server | Yes |
| X3 | A session's codec from the server's log: H.264 by default, HEVC only when allowed and the phone can decode it (§2 C1) | The OPPO, a throwaway server | Yes |
| X4 | No clipboard echo loop: the clipboard both ways with the sync turned off and on, through the throwaway's clipboard stand-in (§7) | The OPPO, a throwaway server | Yes |

## Not in it

- T9, a second screen and one window on GNOME and KDE: it needs one of those desktops to build against (the T cycle's end entry has its mapping).
- V8's home-again case, and the HUD presets (§11b): they need the phone away and home again, or eyes on the picture.
- The owner-gated list carries over: the phone as a webcam, one-time codes, picture messages and group texts, a browser link for devices without the app, receiving from Quick Share, and unlocking with the phone's fingerprint.
