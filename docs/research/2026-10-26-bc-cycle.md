# The BC cycle (2026-10-26), after BB

AX to AZ made a computer removed on the phone forget what the phone kept for it, and BB made the phone hear a computer that unpaired it. This cycle audits every other way a saved computer leaves or changes on the phone: the doctor's Pair again, a session to a found computer that never pairs, a wake relay, the texts queue, and the bridge's own leftovers. The audit found the doctor's path skipping most of Remove, a removed computer still sending other cards' wake packets, certificate pins with no card to remove, and texts queued for one computer sent to the next. Each item is its own PR (Phase R). The plan is removed when the cycle ends.

| # | Item | Where | Checkable here |
|---|---|---|---|
| BC1 | The doctor's Pair again says it removes the computer but skips most of Remove: the link to it stays up (AX1), the deck widget keeps its buttons, and its "also send to" pick stays, so a later pairing sends it notification copies unchosen.
