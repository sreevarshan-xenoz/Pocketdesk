## Where we are

- **The phone and the computer reach each other for nearly everything.** That covers sessions, the remote, casts that the computer can drive, files and folders, the clipboard with history, notifications with answers and clears, texts, calls, dialing, commands, the deck, widgets and tiles, and the state of each side.
- **Gaps the repo shows, each checked against the code for this plan:**
  - **Notifications wait for the phone's screen on some phones.** The L cycle's checks on the OPPO saw ColorOS freeze the app's background threads with the screen off, even with battery optimization lifted. Pushes then waited for the screen. The Health card (D1) checks battery optimization but has no check for a frozen app. Nothing in `doctor/` looks at it.
  - **The phone does not see what each computer allows it** (L7 part 2, `2026-10-21-both-sides-design.md`). A new pairing still gets the default permissions with no choice.
  - **Image copies cross only during a session.**
    - `server/src/clipboard_push.rs`: "Text only: an image needs a transfer that dials the phone back."
    - The phone's Send clipboard tile sends text only (`clip/ClipboardSend.kt`).
    - N4's image road runs only inside a session, but the background file road (T10d, 2c) has existed since.
  - **The computer's keyboard reaches the phone only through a cast.** C8's `ControlRunner` runs "from the cast being shown to its end". The Accessibility service can type at a focused field, but nothing asks it to without a cast.
  - **Photos sent from the phone lose where they were taken.** The phone app never asks for `ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION` and never calls `MediaStore.setRequireOriginal`. Android 10+ therefore removes the GPS tags from what Pocketdesk reads, by share, folder send, recent photos (C7) or the drive (D8).
  - **A phone notification toasts on the computer while the owner is holding the phone.** Nothing in `notify/` reads the screen state (`isInteractive`).
  - **The phone's sound needs its picture** (K5 part 2, `2026-10-20-phone-sound-design.md`). The desktop opens the cast's window as a cast starts.
  - **`tel:` links on the computer go nowhere** (L4 part 2). There is no `RegisteredApplications` entry or `x-scheme-handler/tel` file, and no Call on a call's note.
  - **Home Assistant cannot send a phone command** (L6 part 2, "Left out" in `2026-10-21-phone-command-design.md`).
  - **A copy on the phone still needs a tap** (K8 was never spiked).
- **Owed checks:** 773 open (`docs/owed-checks.md`), 460 of them runnable here. M does not add a batch of its own: device checks stay batched for the owner, as in L.

The owner's standing rules hold:
- spec, then TDD; one logical change per commit;
- a fresh review per item, its findings fixed one commit each;
- gates per change; device checks batched for the owner;
- docs with each change; knobs in Settings on both sides;
- no macOS or iOS;
- no power, lock or server-stop actions on the owner's computer, and no driver installs;
- nothing that needs a SIM, unless it is marked owed.

## M1. Linked with the screen off

**What:** first, find out what freezes Pocketdesk on the OPPO with its screen off. Then:
- **Detect it:**
  - the background link's own ticker notices gaps it never slept through (`elapsedRealtime` jumping between ticks while the process is alive);
  - the computer notices a linked phone that stops answering pings while its last report said "screen off".
- **Say it:**
  - The phone's Health card (D1) adds "Android or the phone's maker paused Pocketdesk with the screen off". It names the maker's setting where it is known (ColorOS: allow background activity and auto launch; Samsung: never sleeping apps) and opens that page when an intent reaches it, else dontkillmyapp's page.
  - The computer's Overview shows such a phone as "asleep: notifications wait for its screen" instead of "linked".
- **Work around it where measuring shows a way.** Candidates, each measured on the OPPO before any is kept:
  - a foreground service type `connectedDevice` for the background link;
  - an exact alarm while idle that lets the link run briefly;
  - a high-priority wake from the computer over UDP.

**Why:** KDE Discuss 2026-04-30 and 2026-06-10, the 2025-09-03 GSConnect comparison, KDE bug 392950, and the L cycle's own checks (2026-10-21).

**Touches:**
- **The phone:** `doctor/` (a `FrozenCheck`, JVM-tested on recorded tick gaps) and the link keeper.
- **The computer:** a phone state on the Overview and in `devices --json`.

**Checked here:** on the OPPO with the screen off (`adb shell input keyevent 26`):
- `adb shell dumpsys activity processes` and `/proc/<pid>/status` for the frozen state;
- a push's delay from the computer (the throwaway chat app's notification timed in the event log), before and after each candidate;
- the Health card read back with `uiautomator dump`.

**Owed:** a Samsung and a Pixel (other hardware); a night on battery without USB.

**Risks:**
- **There may be no fix.** ColorOS's freezer is the maker's and may ignore every exemption an app can take. Then M1 ships the detection and the words only, and says so.
- **Battery:** any workaround is measured with N7's script for an hour, and is dropped if it costs more than it saves.
- **USB keeps the phone from sleeping** (as N7 found). The real run needs the phone unplugged and adb over Wi-Fi.

## M2. Both sides' switches, part 2: the phone's mirror and "Whose phone is it?"

**What:**
- **The mirror on the phone.** Each computer's menu gets "What this computer allows": each feature with this computer's permission, its Settings switch, and the phone's own switch. A feature that is held back says which side holds it ("Turn on Files for this phone on the computer"). The computer answers the phone's switches report (L7 part 1) with the permissions and settings it gives that phone, as booleans over L7's fixed list.
- **Presets at pairing.** The desktop's Pair page asks "Whose phone is it?":
  - "Mine": the permissions as now;
  - "Someone else's": files and links only, with no input, clipboard, notifications, texts, calls, commands or ringing;
  - "A guest": I3's guest pairing, for 15 minutes to a day.

  The phone's Pair confirmation shows what was given.

**Why:** KDE Discuss 2026-10-17 (profiles for a spouse's phone) and 2026-07-20 (settings at pairing).

**Touches:** both:
- a reply packet to `kdeconnect.pocketdesk.switches`;
- the phone's menu (`SwitchReport` gets a mirror model, JVM-tested);
- the Pair page and the trust store's defaults by preset.

**Checked here:**
- unit tests for the mirror's rows and each preset's permissions;
- the C4 harness for the Pair page's choice;
- the OPPO paired to a throwaway server as "Someone else's": the mirror read back by `uiautomator dump`, and a clipboard push refused.

**Risks:**
- **Telling the phone what the computer allows is new information.** It is booleans only, and only for that phone.
- **A preset is a starting point, not a lock.** Changing a permission later on Devices is unaffected, and the mirror follows within a report.
- **A KDE device pairs through its own flow (3b).** The preset applies only to Pocketdesk phones; say so on the page.

## M3. Image copies without a session, both ways

**What:**
- **Computer → phone.** When the computer's clipboard holds an image and no text, the server offers it to each linked phone with Clipboard on, over the background file road (T10d, or 2c's stream on the internet road). The offer is marked as a clipboard image. The phone's listener fetches it and puts it on its clipboard through N4's FileProvider.
- **Phone → computer.** The Send clipboard tile and "To computer" send an image as N4's `clipboard_image`, outside a session too.

Settings:
- the computer: "Images to phones' clipboards", off by default;
- the phone: "Computer's copied images in the background", off by default.

Both have N4's limits: 32 MiB and 40 megapixels. An image goes once per change, and never a copy marked sensitive.

**Why:**
- Phone Link copies images, resizing those over 1 MB;
- KDE's work-in-progress MR 221;
- GSConnect issue 678;
- Microsoft's 2026 clipboard history prototype.

**Touches:**
- **The computer:** `clipboard_push.rs` learns images (Windows' PNG or `CF_DIB`, `wl-paste --type image/png`). D3's history keeps a line for an image, not the image itself.
- **The phone:** the listener's applier and the tile.

**Checked here:**
- server tests through a new image seam beside `POCKETDESK_CLIPBOARD_FILE` (a PNG file in place of the clipboard);
- the OPPO: the image's SHA-256 read back by a throwaway app, and the phone → computer direction by that app copying a PNG and tapping the tile;
- nothing sent with either switch off.

**Owed:** a real paste on both sides, and Linux.

**Risks:**
- **Screenshots copied all day go to the phone in the background.** That costs data and battery, so the switch is off by default, the image goes on Wi-Fi only, and its size is shown in Settings.
- **The ping-pong:** N4's `ImageClipRule` keeps an image from bouncing back. Reuse it, and test it across both roads.
- **Android 13+ shows its clipboard overlay for each image put there.** That is expected, and is said in Settings.

## M4. Type on the phone from the computer's keyboard

**What:** "Type on the phone" on each phone in the tray and on the Overview, and a key in desktop Settings (H3's registration, off by default). Each opens a small window that takes the computer's keyboard:
- **text** is typed at the phone's focused field;
- **Enter, Backspace, Tab, arrows** go as keys;
- **Back, Home and Recents** go as C8's actions.

The phone's control Accessibility service does it without a cast. No keyboard switch is needed on the phone, unlike KDE's IME, which is where the May 2026 thread got stuck. While the window is open, the phone shows an ongoing notification, "The computer is typing here", with Stop.

**Why:**
- KDE bug 370919;
- KDE Discuss 2026-05-07 to 05-09 (the IME dance), 2026-08-05 (a global shortcut) and 2026-02-22 (Home from remote input).

**Touches:**
- **The computer:** a typing window (egui) and a `kdeconnect.pocketdesk.type` packet on the control link. It needs the phone's per-phone Input permission and C8's desktop switch.
- **The phone:** `ControlService` takes typing outside a cast, under C8's "Let the computer control this phone". It types only from its own computer, never into a password field (C8's rule), and at C8's rate.

**Checked here:**
- JVM tests for the rule (outside a cast, password fields, rate, own computer);
- the OPPO with a scratch field in a throwaway app: text, a Backspace after typing (C8's cached-field bug) and Enter, read back by `uiautomator dump`;
- the window driven through the C4 harness, not by this box's keyboard.

**Owed:** the hotkey pressed by hand (input on this box), and Linux.

**Risks:**
- **Typing into a screen nobody sees.** The notification says it, the window names the phone and the field's app, and the phone stops at once if it locks. A locked phone takes nothing (C8's rule).
- **Android 17's Advanced Protection revokes the service,** as for C8. The window then says so.
- **Non-Latin text:** `ACTION_SET_TEXT` sets a field's whole text, as C8 does, so scripts and emoji go as text, not keys. Test Tamil.

## M5. Photos keep where they were taken

**What:** phone Settings "Photos keep where they were taken", off by default. With it on, the phone asks for `ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION` and reads every photo it sends through `MediaStore.setRequireOriginal`, so the GPS tags arrive. That covers the share sheet, folder sends, recent photos (C7), the phone's folders (T10g) and the drive (D8). With it off, nothing changes, and the setting's line says Android removes the location.

**Why:** KDE bug 421613; the /e/ forum's diagnosis; LocalSend holding the permission.

**Touches:** the phone only (the readers in `service/`, `files/`, `ShareActivity`).

**Checked here:**
- JVM tests for which URIs can be asked for the original (MediaStore ones; SAF and other providers fall back);
- a JPEG with GPS tags pushed to `DCIM/Camera` by adb, then:
  - shared by an intent, and the tags read on the computer (a test helper with an EXIF reader);
  - sent with the switch off, the tags gone;
  - fetched through C7, the tags kept.

**Risks:**
- **A photo's place is personal.** The switch is off by default, the line says who receives it, and SECURITY.md gains a limit.
- **Another provider decides for itself.** A cloud gallery's provider (Google Photos) may still strip the tags. Say "when the photo is on the phone".
- **SAF folder picks** may still be redacted. Measure, and say what was found.

## M6. No toast while the phone is in hand

**What:** phone Settings "Quiet on the computer while I use the phone", off by default. While the phone's screen is on and unlocked, each notification is sent marked `phoneInUse`, and the computer shows it on its Notifications page and in Activity without a toast or sound. Calls (F5) always toast.

**Why:** KDE's Android MR 257, "post notification only if the screen is off". Unlike that MR, the notification still reaches the computer's page, so nothing is lost.

**Touches:**
- **The phone:** `NotificationForwardRule` reads `PowerManager.isInteractive` and `KeyguardManager.isKeyguardLocked`.
- **The computer:** the toast path skips one marked so, as K2a's pause does. A computer from before M6 ignores the field and toasts as now.

**Checked here:**
- JVM tests;
- the throwaway chat app with the OPPO's screen on and unlocked, then off (`input keyevent 26`);
- the toast seen or not through the server's toast log and the event's `phone_in_use` field.

**Risks:**
- **A phone left unlocked on the desk keeps the computer quiet.** Show "Quiet: you are on the phone" on the Overview while it holds.
- **It overlaps a later "toast only on the computer in use"** (left out of L8 part 2). The rules would compose: either one quiets the toast.

## M7. The phone's sound without its screen (K5 part 2)

**What:** "Play the phone's sound here" in each computer's menu, alone. The phone captures what its apps play under a MediaProjection, as K5 part 1 does, but sends no picture: a cast session with an audio-only flag. The computer plays it with K5's player (`server/src/phone_sound.rs`) and opens no window. The Sessions page shows "the phone's sound" with Stop.

**Why:**
- scrcpy's `--no-video`;
- SoundDrift and AudioRelay;
- K5's own plan, which left this as part 2 (`2026-10-20-phone-sound-design.md`).

**Touches:**
- **The phone:** `CastService` without the encoder, keeping `CastSound`.
- **The computer:** the cast relay accepts an audio-only cast, and the desktop opens no window for it.

**Checked here:**
- the throwaway tone app on the OPPO;
- the WAV seam (`POCKETDESK_PHONE_AUDIO_FILE`) checked for the tone's frequency, level and delay, as in K5;
- no cast window opened (the harness, and the relay's log).

**Owed:** by ear, and a real music app.

**Risks:**
- **Android 14 asks consent for each projection,** with the screen named even though no picture goes. The phone says "Android calls this screen sharing; only the sound is sent".
- **The phone's speaker keeps playing,** as in part 1.
- **Battery:** no encoder runs, but the projection does. Measure an hour.

## M8. `tel:` links and Call back (L4 part 2)

**What:**
- **Windows:** desktop Settings "Open phone numbers on the phone". It writes a `RegisteredApplications` entry under HKCU for `tel:` and `callto:`. The owner picks Pocketdesk in Default apps, which Windows requires to be done by hand.
- **Linux:** an `x-scheme-handler/tel` desktop file.

A `tel:` link then runs `pocketdesk call` with the number. With one phone it goes there; with several, the desktop app asks which, as J5 does for drops. Also **Call back** on a call's note (F5) and on Activity's call lines, through L4's road.

**Why:** KDE bugs 341950, 397333 and 410149 (2026-06-13), and KDE's own `tel:` handler.

**Touches:** the computer (the server's CLI parsing a `tel:` URI with percent-escapes and visual separators, the install code beside the Send To menu, and the Notifications page). The phone is unchanged.

**Checked here:**
- unit tests for `tel:` parsing (L4's number rule, never `*` or `#`);
- the registry keys written and read back, and removed by `uninstall`;
- `pocketdesk call tel:+1-555-0100` to the OPPO through a throwaway server, the dialer opening (`dumpsys activity activities`);
- the Linux file through `desktop-file-validate` in CI.

**Owed:** the Default apps pick and a link clicked in a browser, by hand; a real call (a SIM).

**Risks:**
- **HKCU writes,** as the Send To menu's: offered only from Settings, and undone by `uninstall` and by the switch.
- **A web page can offer a `tel:` link.** The phone's dialer still waits for the owner's tap (L4's rule).

## M9. Home Assistant buttons for phone commands (L6 part 2)

**What:** desktop Settings "Phone commands" (`[[phone_command]]`: a label, a phone, a name and a text). Each becomes a button on Home Assistant through 1e's discovery. A press sends L6's `kdeconnect.pocketdesk.command` through `commands_out/`, held to the phone's Command permission and L6's pace.

**Why:** L6's spec left it out. KDE Discuss 2026-08-05 asks for command buttons; Join and AutoRemote exist for this.

**Touches:** the computer only (`home_assistant.rs`, the config, Settings).

**Checked here:**
- unit tests for the entities;
- a local broker as in 1e: a press published and the command seen at the OPPO's throwaway receiver in logcat;
- a retained or empty message pressing nothing (1e's review).

**Owed:** Home Assistant itself; Tasker.

**Risks:**
- **Anyone who can press buttons in Home Assistant can trigger the phone's automations.** Say it in SECURITY.md beside limits 19 and 38.

## M10. Copies on the phone sent by themselves (K8, a spike first)

**What:** K8's plan, unchanged, with one change in what the spike tries first. The thread's latest reply (2026-09) suggests the Accessibility route, which C8's service could take. The spike is an hour by adb on the OPPO (Android 14) and decides:
- **The log route:** does `logcat` with `READ_LOGS` still work from the background? Android 13's LogcatManager is said to ask the owner and deny background readers. The thread reports this failing on Android 16.
- **The Accessibility route:** does a copy raise an event the control service can see?
- **The read:** does an invisible window get focus to read the clipboard from the background?

If no way works on Android 14 without root or Shizuku, M10 ends as a note in the plan saying why, and nothing ships.

**Why:** KDE Discuss "KDE Connect ClipBoard sync", 2023-10-24 to 2026-09-06; the Shizuku builds users pass around.

**Touches:** the phone only, if it ships.

**Checked here:** by adb only. A throwaway app copies text, and the computer's clipboard seam receives it; a copy marked sensitive does not go.

**Risks:**
- **Widening the Accessibility service** raises what Android 17's Advanced Protection revokes and what F-Droid and Play review. It needs its own switch, off by default.
- **Battery:** an event filter running all day. Measure with N7's script.
- **A future Android may close the route.**

### M10's spike, 2026-10-21: begun, its device hour owed

Nothing ships yet. The hour by adb on the OPPO stopped after its first try: the phone showed the owner's App Installer screen (Pocketdesk 0.5.0 just installed), so it was left alone. What stands:

- **The log route is closed on Android 13 and later.** LogcatManager asks the owner each time an app reads the device's logs, and refuses an app in the background. The thread reports it failing on 16 too. Not tried further.
- **The Accessibility route is the one left.** Watching events (`uiautomator events`) during a copy recorded only the window change. The keystrokes went to the installer's screen, not a field, so the copy's own events are untested. The open question is whether ColorOS raises any event for a copy that C8's service can see: Android 13's clipboard overlay window, or a click on a Copy item.
- **The read:** an app with a bound Accessibility service is exempt from Android's limits on starting activities from the background. So a see-through activity that takes focus, reads the clipboard once and finishes is the read to try once a copy is seen.

**Owed, with the owner:** the hour itself on the OPPO: a copy by keys in a scratch field, the events it raises, then the see-through read. If either fails on Android 14, M10 ends here.

## Considered and left out

- **K6, a link for a browser** (LocalSend's "Share via link"):
  - It needs a LAN listener and a firewall rule on this box, which is the owner's OK and not done by script.
  - Guest pairing (I3) covers sharing with a phone that has Pocketdesk.
  - LocalSend does it well already.
  - It stays on the list for when the owner OKs the firewall rule.
- **The phone's volume and ringer from the computer** (KDE Discuss 2025-10-04; Microsoft's 2026 flyout with vibrate): Android 17 ignores volume calls from background apps (N4), so it would break on the next Android. D2's Do Not Disturb already covers "silence it".
- **Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or hotspot toggles from the computer** (same thread): Android gives ordinary apps no way to toggle them since Android 10 and 13.
- **Batteries of the phone's Bluetooth devices** (KDE Discuss 2026-08-05): the level is a system-only API.
- **The phone's GPS as the computer's location** (KDE bug 461545): Windows needs a location driver, which is a driver install. Linux's GeoClue could take NMEA, but that is Linux only and not checkable here.
- **Approve `sudo` or polkit with the phone's fingerprint** (KDE bug 398931, comments to 2025-11): it is a PAM security component, Linux only.
- **Folder sync** (KDE Discuss 2025-10-04, 2026-04-15): conflicts make it a product of its own, and Syncthing exists.
- **Files on the clipboard** (copy in Explorer, paste on the phone; KDE Discuss 2026-10-06): after M3, if asked again. Drops (L2) and Send To (T10e) cover the case.
- **RCS** (KDE bug 464654, 2026-09-15): no API.
- **Call audio on the computer** (bug 410149): Android lets no app capture it, and Bluetooth hands-free is the system's own.
- **Pocketdesk on Android TV** (KDE Discuss 2026-09-06): no TV here, and it is phase 5.
- **Desktop notifications to the phone on Windows:** this needs a package identity (C9, set aside). Linux already has it.
- **Phone Link's Start menu feed:** the Activity page and the tray (D5) cover it.
