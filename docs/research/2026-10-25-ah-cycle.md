# The AH cycle (2026-10-25), after AG

The AA to AG cycles ran nearly every owed check this computer and the OPPO can reach. Two of them ended at something the product did not say: why an image from the computer was not taken (AD1), and how long a crop's rebuild stalls the stream (AE3). This cycle makes the product say both, and checks them (Phase R: what exists, made visible). It removes this plan when it ends.

Each item is a branch and a PR, with its design and review in the PR and its entry in `docs/status-history.md`.

| # | Item | Where | Checkable here |
|---|---|---|---|
| AH1 | The phone logs an image from the computer taken onto its clipboard (its size, never the image), or why a fetch failed, as AA1 did for text; the runner's `ad1-image-copy` then requires the line | The OPPO, a throwaway server | Yes |
| AH2 | The session record gains the encoder's rebuild times (`rebuild_n`, its p50 to max), kept apart from the frames' encodes, so a crop's stall shows (§11); the runner's `ae2-crops` then reports and requires them | This computer, the OPPO | Yes |
