# The BD cycle (2026-10-26), after BC

AX to BC made the phone forget what it kept for a computer that left or changed. This cycle audits the same on a computer, for the owner's other computers (2f): one forgotten on the Computers page, unpaired the other way, paired again at new addresses, or a server restart. The audit found a stale Computers entry taking over sends to a computer since paired the other way, rows left "waiting" for good after a restart, and pairing's last outcome and old pins outliving the computer. Each item is its own PR (Phase R). The plan is removed when the cycle ends.
